<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
**Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship, UCF — May 2024 to August 2024**
</div>

Computational fluid dynamics analysis of a Formula 2 front wing, aimed at reducing drag and increasing downforce.
<br>

<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
Project role - **Computational Fluid Dynamics Intern**
</div>

- Ran CFD analysis in STAR-CCM+ to optimize the front wing design.
- Modeled airflow, absolute pressure, and the lambda-2 criterion to evaluate vortex structures.
- Turned the results into design recommendations for reduced drag and enhanced downforce.
