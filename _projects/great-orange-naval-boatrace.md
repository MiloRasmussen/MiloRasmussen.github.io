**Great Orange Naval Boatrace, UCF**
</div>

A team of three designed and built a structurally sound vessel for a semi-circular pond race. It placed 8th among more than 200 entries.
<br>

<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
Project role - **Visual Design Lead**
</div>

- Led the visual design of the boat, recreating the Titanic from carved and assembled Styrofoam.
- Crafted historically accurate details, including the handrails.
