**Computational Fluids and Aerodynamics Lab (NASA affiliated), UCF — March 2023 to March 2026**
</div>

Three years of research on Formula 2 car aerodynamics, from simulation to experimental hardware.
<br>

<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
Project role - **Research Assistant**
</div>

- Developed CAD models in SolidWorks and ran CFD simulations in STAR-CCM+ to study Formula 2 car aerodynamics.
- Configured a Raspberry Pi with an MCC 172 DAQ HAT and wrote Python scripts for analog data acquisition, actuator control, and real-time sensor data collection for experimental setups.
- Presented the findings at university and regional conferences to audiences of 100+ attendees.
