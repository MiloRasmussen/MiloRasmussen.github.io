<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
**Eagle Scout Project**
</div>

A citrus grove at a middle school, built to benefit the local community and support agricultural education. The project was part of more than 150 community service hours over three years, and I earned the rank of Eagle Scout shortly after.
<br>

<div class="section-header" markdown="1">
Project role - **Project Lead**
</div>

- Managed every phase of the project: planning, budgeting, material sourcing, and coordinating 10+ volunteers.
- Planted 12 citrus trees and installed an irrigation system.
- Laid mulch and built a brick border to finish the grove.
